3.1 Prior Knowledge - before joining

HSMAs can come from a wide variety of backgrounds.

A large number join with zero prior programming knowldge.

However, you will also get

  • people with extensive R knowledge but limited or no Python experience
  • people with a small amount of prior R or Python experience
  • people (usually analysts) who have used complex Excel and SQL, but have not used a language like R or Python before.

HSMAs can be

  • analysts working in the NHS or related organisations
  • clinicians
  • managers
  • GPs

There are no particular entry requirements for HSMA beyond a self declaration of a reasonable level of computer literacy. We do not expect students to have any particular mathematical, statistical, or other knowledge.

Therefore, sessions have to be designed with this diversity in mind; you will often need to cover the foundations in significant detail.

Tip

In HSMA, we do not necessarily shy away from explaining more technical thing - but it’s important to consider creative approaches to doing so, like games, analogies, etc.

However, in some cases, it may also be reasonable to keep a technical explanation extremely simplified or high level. The main goal is to make students into safe, effective users of various techniques; if deep understanding of the technical underpinnings of a method is not necessary to achieve that, then consider skipping it in favour of spending more time on potential pitfalls.
